# Credora V1 Known Limitations

Credora V1 is intentionally conservative. It prioritizes evidence integrity, user voice, and transparent local tooling over automation claims it cannot verify.

## Current limitations

- Credora does not publish to LinkedIn automatically in V1. Final posting remains manual.
- Credora does not scrape private LinkedIn data or bypass platform controls.
- Fact-checking quality depends on the sources supplied or retrieved by the host environment; unsupported claims must remain labelled unsupported or unverifiable.
- The local quality score is an editorial heuristic, not an AI-content detector and not a prediction of reach or engagement.
- Hook suggestions are editorial starting points, not performance guarantees.
- Performance-review conclusions are confidence-limited by sample size and confounders. Small histories should not be treated as causal evidence.
- Voice matching improves only when the user provides representative writing samples. Credora must not invent a voice profile from insufficient evidence.
- Content-memory validation checks record structure, not whether stored claims are factually true.
- Research-post mode can preserve source caveats, but it cannot recover limitations that are absent from the supplied evidence.
- Platform-specific limits, timing advice, and algorithm claims must not be presented as universal facts unless verified from current authoritative evidence.

## V1 safety boundary

Credora should never fabricate metrics, clients, revenue, credentials, citations, quotes, study details, sample sizes, effect sizes, or outcomes. When evidence is missing, the correct state is uncertainty—not invention.
