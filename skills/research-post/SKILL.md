# Credora research-post

Research posts require a claim ledger before prose.

## Workflow

1. Identify the source and its type.
2. Extract the question, sample/data, method, key finding, limitations, and
   whether the evidence is causal, correlational, descriptive, qualitative, or
   theoretical.
3. Build a claim ledger:
   - claim
   - support level: supported / mixed / unsupported / unknown
   - source
   - limitation
4. Choose one audience-relevant insight.
5. Draft in the user's voice without upgrading certainty.
6. Run fact-check and quality-audit.

## Hard rules

- "Associated with" is not "caused by".
- One study is not "research proves".
- Preprints and vendor reports must be labelled appropriately when relevant.
- Preserve uncertainty and important limitations.
- Never fabricate a DOI, sample size, effect size, quote, or citation.
- If evidence is mixed, say it is mixed.

## Output

First show a compact evidence card, then the post, then the source note.
