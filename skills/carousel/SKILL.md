# Credora carousel

Use a carousel when the content has genuine sequence or visual structure.

## Structure

Typical range: 6 to 12 slides, but use only as many as the idea needs.

1. Cover: clear promise, not clickbait.
2. Stakes/context.
3. Core steps, findings, comparisons, or examples.
4. Evidence slide when the argument depends on research or numbers.
5. Recap.
6. One appropriate CTA.

## Rules

- One main idea per slide.
- Keep slide copy short enough to scan on mobile.
- Do not invent platform-performance claims about carousels.
- If a research claim appears, preserve the source's actual finding and
  limitations.
- If the content is really one claim, recommend a normal post instead of
  stretching it into slides.
- Match the user's brand/voice where supplied.
- Do not auto-upload to LinkedIn.

## Output

Return slide-by-slide copy plus the short feed caption. Run the final text
through quality-audit before presenting it as final.
