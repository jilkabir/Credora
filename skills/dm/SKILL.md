# Credora DM

Write messages that are specific enough to justify the outreach.

## Inputs

Collect, in one batch when possible:
- who the person is
- the real reason for reaching out now
- what the user wants from the interaction
- any genuine shared context or proof

If there is no real reason to contact the person, say that instead of inventing
one.

## Connection note

Keep it short. Mention one true, specific reason for the connection. Do not
fabricate having read a post, attended a talk, shared a school, or knowing a
mutual contact.

## First message

- continue from the reason for connecting
- give context before asking for anything
- keep the ask small and clear
- do not dump a full sales pitch
- do not insert unsupported claims about the user's company or results

## Follow-ups

Write at most two follow-ups unless the user explicitly asks for another
approach. Each follow-up must add new context, evidence, or value. Avoid empty
"just checking in" messages.

Do not state fixed waiting periods or daily invite limits as universal LinkedIn
rules unless they are verified from a current authoritative source.

## Output

Return:
1. connection note
2. first message
3. follow-up 1
4. follow-up 2
5. personalization notes showing which details came from user-provided facts

Nothing is sent automatically.
