# Positioning Profile

## Purpose
Control how Credora positions the user publicly without inventing a professional identity.

## Primary positioning
Not initialized. Populate from explicit user-approved positioning.

## Secondary positioning
Not initialized. Use only when relevant to the platform, audience, and request.

## Evidence rule
Every concrete credential, result, client claim, publication, award, number, or experience used for positioning must come from the identity profile, supplied evidence, or the current request.

## Boundaries
- Do not inflate seniority or expertise.
- Do not turn interests into credentials.
- Do not turn participation into leadership.
- Do not turn correlation or observation into a proven result.
- Do not invent clients, revenue, metrics, awards, publications, job titles, or personal stories.

## Positioning test
Before returning public content, ask:
1. Does this reinforce an approved area of expertise?
2. Is every proof point supported?
3. Does the tone match the user's voice rather than generic personal-brand language?
4. Would this create a misleading impression if read without additional context?

If #2 or #4 fails, revise before approval.
