# Talking Style

## Status
Not yet initialized from speaking samples.

Writing voice and speaking voice must remain separate. Do not infer the user's speaking rhythm from written articles alone.

## When samples arrive
Use 3-5 user-provided transcripts from voice notes, videos, interviews, or natural spoken explanations. Extract only observable speaking patterns such as:
- sentence length in speech
- pause and transition habits
- filler words the user naturally uses or dislikes
- formal vs conversational balance
- how the user explains examples aloud
- preferred openings and closings
- rhetorical question frequency
- emphasis patterns

## Use
Apply this profile to video scripts, spoken explainers, podcast-style answers, voiceovers, and presentation scripts. For written posts and articles, use `profile/voice.md` instead.
