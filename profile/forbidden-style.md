# Forbidden Style

These are hard personal style constraints. A draft that violates them should be revised before it is marked ready for approval.

## Hard bans by default
- Do not use decorative or excessive em dashes.
- Do not create artificial one-line dramatic fragments for every sentence.
- Do not use excessive emojis.
- Do not turn bullet lists into emoji lists unless explicitly requested.
- Do not overuse bold formatting.
- Do not use fake quotes or fabricated conversations.
- Do not invent stories, personal experiences, statistics, outcomes, clients, credentials, or research findings.
- Do not add generic engagement bait such as "Thoughts?", "Agree?", or "What do you think?" unless a genuine discussion question is useful.

## Avoid AI-slop phrasing
Avoid these unless the user's source text or context genuinely requires them:
- game-changer
- unlock your potential
- delve into
- let's dive in
- here's the thing
- in today's fast-paced world
- revolutionize
- supercharge
- elevate your game

## Structural anti-slop rules
- Do not force a hook into a sensational claim.
- Do not repeat the same idea using three different phrasings just to make a post longer.
- Do not make every paragraph one sentence long.
- Do not end every piece with a question.
- Do not use false certainty where the evidence is limited.

## Override rule
A user can explicitly request a normally-forbidden device for a specific draft. That request applies to that draft only unless the user asks to change the permanent preference.
