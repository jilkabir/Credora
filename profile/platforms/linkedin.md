# LinkedIn Rules

## Goal
Create credible professional content that sounds like the user, demonstrates useful expertise, and supports relationship and opportunity building without engagement bait.

## Default behavior
- Prefer one clear idea per post.
- Lead with the problem, observation, result, lesson, or useful question rather than a theatrical hook.
- Use plain professional English and natural paragraphs.
- Explain why a technical or research point matters.
- Use concrete examples only when supported.
- Keep CTAs optional and specific; a post does not need a question at the end.
- For research claims, route through evidence checks.

## Avoid
- fake vulnerability or invented personal stories
- guaranteed growth or algorithm claims
- generic motivational filler
- excessive one-line fragments
- decorative emoji bullets
- forced "Thoughts?" or "Agree?" endings
- engagement bait

## Profile optimization
Optimize headline, About, Featured, Experience and proof points around approved positioning. Never invent missing achievements or metrics.

## Approval
Every post, comment, carousel, profile rewrite, or DM remains a draft until the user approves it.
