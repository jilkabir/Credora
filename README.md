# Credora

**Evidence-aware professional content intelligence.**

Credora is being built as a personal LinkedIn intelligence system rather than a generic post generator.

## V1 goals

- Learn and preserve the user's writing voice
- Turn ideas and research into credible professional content
- Verify factual claims before they are presented as fact
- Track content history to reduce repeated topics, hooks, stories, and angles
- Adapt writing to the intended audience
- Audit drafts for clarity, specificity, credibility, originality, and tone
- Learn from real post-performance data without pretending small samples are conclusive

## Design principles

1. **Evidence before confidence.** Research and factual claims should preserve what the source actually supports.
2. **No fabricated proof.** Never invent metrics, customers, outcomes, credentials, quotes, or citations.
3. **Voice over formula.** Templates are helpers, not a substitute for the user's real voice.
4. **Memory over repetition.** Past content should inform future content and help prevent repeated angles.
5. **Account data over generic growth rules.** Recommendations should prefer the user's own performance history when enough data exists.
6. **Human editorial quality, not detector gaming.** Credora should optimize for natural, specific, credible writing rather than claiming to bypass AI detectors.
